## CHAPTER REVISION SEQUENCING (INITIATING WITH BUYBACK OF SECURITIES)

### 1. Priority Starter Chapter: Buyback of Securities (Section 68, 69 & 70 — Companies Act, 2013)
- **Why Start Here**: Self-contained 5-to-10 mark numerical + journal entry question in almost every ICAI attempt; zero dependency on other chapters.
- **Sources of Buyback (Sec 68(1))**: Free Reserves, Securities Premium Account, or Proceeds of the issue of any shares or other specified securities *(Trap: Cannot use proceeds of an earlier issue of the SAME kind of shares)*.
- **Preliminary Condition**: Shares being bought back MUST be **fully paid-up**. If partly paid, call and receive the final call before passing the buyback entry.

#### The 3-Test Statutory Limit Matrix (Maximum Permissible Buyback — Apply Lowest of the 3)
| Col 1: Statutory Test / Provision | Col 2: Formula & Limit (Recognition / Computation) | Col 3: Examiner Trap & Compliance Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Test 1: Resource Test (Sec 68(2)(c))** | Max Buyback Value <= **25% of (Total Paid-up Capital + Free Reserves)** | Free Reserves include **Securities Premium** for Sec 68; Board Resolution max is **10% of Paid-up Equity + Free Reserves**. |
| **Test 2: Share Outstanding Test** | Max No. of Equity Shares bought back in a FY <= **25% of Total Paid-up Equity Shares** | Check **number of shares** (not total capital value); applies per financial year. |
| **Test 3: Debt-Equity Ratio Test (Sec 68(2)(d))** | Post-Buyback **Secured + Unsecured Debt <= 2 * Post-Buyback (Paid-up Capital + Free Reserves)** | **Crucial Trap**: Deduct both Buyback Payout AND **CRR transfer (Sec 69)** from Free Reserves when solving the simultaneous equation! |
| **Capital Redemption Reserve (Sec 69)** | Transfer **Nominal Value of shares bought back out of Free Reserves/SP** to CRR | Nominal value bought back = **Proceeds of Fresh Issue (Preference) + Transfer to CRR**; CRR can only be used for **fully paid bonus shares**. |
| **Time Limits & Cooling Period (Sec 68(4)/(8))** | Complete within **1 year** of passing SR/BR; **6-month** cooling-off for same kind of fresh issue | Extinguish & physically destroy bought-back securities within **7 days** of completion; **1-year** gap between two Board-approved buybacks. |

## PART A: COMPANY ACCOUNTS — 3-COLUMN REVISION MATRIX (ICAI MODULE 3)

| Col 1: Chapter & Section | Col 2: Core Accounting / Recognition Rule | Col 3: Key Schedule III Disclosure & Examiner Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Buyback of Securities (Sec 68–70)** | Lowest of: 1) 25% of (Paid-up Cap + FR), 2) 25% of Equity Shares, 3) Post-BB Debt:Equity <= 2:1. | Deduct CRR + Buyback Premium from Free Reserves/SP; partly paid shares cannot be bought back. |
| **Schedule III (Division I — Financial Statements)** | Current vs Non-Current classification based on **12 months** or **Normal Operating Cycle**. | Trade Receivables ageing (<6m, 6m–1yr, 1–2yr, 2–3yr, >3yr) & MSME payable breakup mandatory in Notes. |
| **Internal Reconstruction (Capital Reduction)** | Credit all sacrifices (shareholders/creditors) to **Capital Reduction (Reconstruction) A/c**. | Write off accumulated losses & fictitious assets first; transfer any remaining credit balance to **Capital Reserve**. |
| **Amalgamation of Companies (AS 14)** | **Merger (Pooling of Interests)**: All 5 conditions met -> record at book values; adjust difference in **Reserves**.<br>**Purchase Method**: Record at fair values; difference is **Goodwill or Capital Reserve**. | **Statutory Reserves** (Export Profit / Investment Allowance) in Purchase Method -> Debit *Amalgamation Adjustment Reserve A/c* and Credit *Statutory Reserve A/c*. |
| **Consolidated Financial Statements (AS 21 / 23 / 27)** | **Cost of Control (DoA)**: Compare Investment Cost vs Parent's share of Pre-Acquisition Equity -> **Goodwill / Capital Reserve**. | Eliminate 100% of **Intra-group balances**; eliminate **Unrealized Profit (URP)** on unsold inventory (Downstream: 100% from Parent P&L; Upstream: Parent % from P&L, Minority % from MI). |
| **Branch Accounting (Inland & Foreign Branches)** | **Integral Foreign Operation (IFO)** vs **Non-Integral (NFO)** under AS 11. | **IFO**: Monetary items at closing rate, Non-monetary at historical rate, diff to P&L.<br>**NFO**: All assets/liabilities at closing rate; diff to **Foreign Currency Translation Reserve (FCTR)**. |

## PART B: MASTER 3-COLUMN ACCOUNTING STANDARDS SUMMARY (ALL AS 1 TO AS 29)

| Col 1: AS No., Title & Scope | Col 2: Recognition & Measurement Rule | Col 3: Disclosure & Classic Examiner Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AS 1: Disclosure of Accounting Policies** | **3 Fundamental Assumptions**: Going Concern, Consistency, Accrual. Select policies based on **Prudence, Substance over Form, Materiality**. | If a Fundamental Assumption is followed, **no disclosure needed**; if NOT followed, **fact must be disclosed**; change in policy disclosed with quantified impact. |
| **AS 2: Valuation of Inventories** | Value at **Lower of Cost and Net Realisable Value (NRV)** on an **item-by-item** basis. Fixed production overheads allocated on **Normal Capacity**. | **Raw Materials** are NOT written down below cost if the finished product will be sold **at or above cost** (if FP < Cost, value RM at **Replacement Cost**). Exclude abnormal waste, storage & selling costs. |
| **AS 3: Cash Flow Statements** | Classify into **Operating, Investing, and Financing** activities. Interest/Dividend paid by non-financial entity = **Financing**; received = **Investing**. | For **Financial Enterprises**, interest paid & received and dividend received are **Operating**; dividend paid is **Financing** for ALL entities. Foreign currency unrealized gains/losses are **not** cash flows. |
| **AS 4: Contingencies & Events Occurring After Balance Sheet Date** | **Adjusting Events**: Conditions existed at BS date -> adjust assets/liabilities.<br>**Non-Adjusting Events**: Conditions arose after BS date -> do NOT adjust. | **Exception**: If a post-BS event destroys the **Going Concern** assumption, adjust the financial statements! **Proposed Dividend** is disclosed in Notes (not adjusted) unless declared before BS date. |
| **AS 5: Net Profit or Loss for the Period, Prior Period Items & Changes in Accounting Policies** | **Prior Period Items**: Income/expenses arising from **errors or omissions** in prior periods.<br>**Change in Estimate**: Adjust prospectively in current/future periods. | **Extraordinary Items** (e.g., earthquake loss, attachment of property) must be disclosed **separately** in P&L. Change in depreciation method is now a **Change in Accounting Estimate** (AS 10), not a policy change! |
| **AS 7: Construction Contracts** | Recognize revenue & costs by **Percentage of Completion Method (POCM)**: Completion % = Cost Incurred to Date / Total Estimated Contract Cost. | If **Total Estimated Cost > Total Contract Revenue**, recognize the **100% Expected Loss immediately** as an expense, regardless of whether work has started or stage of completion! |
| **AS 9: Revenue Recognition** | **Sale of Goods**: Risks & rewards transferred + no uncertainty of collection.<br>**Services**: Completed or Proportionate Completion method. | If **uncertainty of ultimate collection** arises *at the time of raising the claim*, **postpone revenue recognition**; if uncertainty arises *after* sale, make a **provision** (do not reverse revenue). |
| **AS 10: Property, Plant & Equipment (PPE)** | Initial recognition at **Cost** (Purchase price + non-refundable taxes + site prep + initial delivery + **PV of decommissioning liability**). Subsequent: **Cost Model** or **Revaluation Model** (by class). | **Bearer Plants** are accounted under AS 10 (harvested produce is excluded). Replacement of a major part is capitalized and the **carrying amount of the replaced part is derecognized**. |
| **AS 11: The Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates** | **Initial**: Transaction date spot rate.<br>**Balance Sheet Date**: Monetary items at **Closing Rate**; Non-monetary at historical/fair value rate. Exchange diff goes to P&L. | **Para 46A Option**: Exchange diff on long-term foreign currency monetary items for **depreciable capital assets** can be added to/deducted from **asset cost** (for other LT items, accumulate in **FCMITDA**). |
| **AS 12: Accounting for Government Grants** | Recognize only when reasonable assurance of compliance & receipt exists.<br>**Depreciable Assets**: Deduct from asset cost OR treat as **Deferred Income**. | **Non-monetary assets** given at concessional rate -> record at acquisition cost; if given **free of cost**, record at **Nominal Value (Re 1)**. **Refund of Grant**: First adjust unamortized Deferred Income, OR add back to asset carrying amount and depreciate prospectively. |
| **AS 13: Accounting for Investments** | **Current Investments**: Lower of Cost and Fair Value (individual or category-wise).<br>**Long-Term Investments**: Carried at **Cost** (reduce only for **other than temporary** decline). | **Right Shares**: If subscribed, add cost to carrying amount; if **renounced (sold)**, sale proceeds go to **P&L** (unless original shares were bought **cum-right** and market price fell below cost after ex-right, then reduce cost). |
| **AS 14: Accounting for Amalgamations** | **Amalgamation in nature of Merger** (All 5 conditions: All assets/liabilities, >= 90% equity shareholders, equity shares for equity, same business, book values) vs **Purchase**. | If even **1 of the 5 conditions fails** (e.g., debentureholders or preference shareholders paid in cash is OK, but equity shareholders paid in cash except fractions fails!), apply **Purchase Method**. |
| **AS 15: Employee Benefits** | **Short-Term**: Undiscounted expense.<br>**Defined Contribution (PF)**: Contribution due.<br>**Defined Benefit (Gratuity)**: **Projected Unit Credit Method (PUCM)** actuarial valuation. | **Actuarial Gains & Losses** must be recognized **immediately in P&L** (cannot be deferred!). **Past Service Cost** is recognized immediately if already vested, or amortized on a straight-line basis until vesting. |
| **AS 16: Borrowing Costs** | Capitalize borrowing costs directly attributable to a **Qualifying Asset** (takes **substantial period**, typically >= 12 months, to get ready). | **General Borrowings**: Apply **Weighted Average Capitalization Rate** to expenditure incurred (capped at actual borrowing cost). **Suspend** during extended active development interruption; **cease** when substantially complete. |
| **AS 17: Segment Reporting** | Identify **Business & Geographical** segments. Reportable if **10% Threshold** met: Revenue (External + Inter-segment) >= 10%, OR Absolute Result >= 10% of higher of combined profit/loss, OR Segment Assets >= 10%. | External revenue of all reportable segments must be **>= 75% of Total Enterprise Revenue**; if less, keep adding segments until 75% is reached (even if they fail the 10% test). Segment Assets/Liabilities **exclude Income Tax & Interest** items. |
| **AS 18: Related Party Disclosures** | Applies to **Control (>50% voting power, board control, or >20% + power to direct)** and **Significant Influence (>= 20% voting power)**, KMP & their relatives. | **No disclosure required** for intra-group transactions in Consolidated Financial Statements, or where disclosure conflicts with **statutory confidentiality** (e.g., Banks). State-controlled enterprises are exempt from disclosing transactions with other state entities. |
| **AS 19: Leases** | **Finance Lease** (transfers substantially all risks & rewards) vs **Operating Lease**.<br>**Lessee (FL)**: Capitalize at **Lower of Fair Value and PV of MLP** (discounted at implicit rate). | **Sale and Leaseback**:<br>• **Finance Lease**: Defer excess of sale proceeds over carrying amount & amortize over lease term.<br>• **Operating Lease**: If Sale Price = Fair Value -> recognize profit/loss immediately; if Sale > FV -> defer excess over FV. |
| **AS 20: Earnings Per Share (EPS)** | **Basic EPS** = Net Profit or Loss for Equity Shareholders / WANES.<br>**Bonus / Split**: Adjust WANES **retrospectively** from beginning of earliest reported period. | **Rights Issue**: Multiply pre-rights shares by Right Factor = Fair Value Cum-Right / Theoretical Ex-Right Fair Value.<br>**Diluted EPS**: Only include potential equity shares that are **Dilutive** (reduce EPS from continuing operations). |
| **AS 21: Consolidated Financial Statements** | Consolidate line-by-line when Parent holds **>50% voting power** OR controls composition of Board. Compute **Cost of Control (DoA)** and **Minority Interest (BS Date)**. | **Exclude Subsidiary** only if: 1) Control is **temporary** (held exclusively for near-future disposal), OR 2) Subsidiary operates under **severe long-term restrictions** impairing fund transfer. |
| **AS 22: Accounting for Taxes on Income** | Tax Expense = Current Tax + Deferred Tax.<br>**Timing Differences** (reverse in future) create DTA/DTL; **Permanent Differences** (e.g., penalties) are ignored. | **Virtual Certainty (supported by convincing evidence)** is mandatory to recognize **DTA** when an enterprise has **unabsorbed depreciation or carry-forward business losses** (otherwise, only **Reasonable Certainty** is needed). |
| **AS 23: Accounting for Investments in Associates in CFS** | Apply **Equity Method** when investor holds **Significant Influence (>= 20% to 50% voting power)**: Record at Cost (identify Goodwill/Capital Reserve) + add/deduct investor's share of post-acquisition profit/loss. | Eliminate **Unrealized Profit (URP)** on transactions between Investor and Associate **only to the extent of the Investor's % share** in the Associate. |
| **AS 24: Discontinuing Operations** | Triggered by binding sale agreement for substantially all assets OR Board approval + formal announcement of detailed plan for a **separate major line of business / geographical area**. | Disclose **Pre-tax Profit/Loss**, Income Tax, Carrying amount of Assets/Liabilities, and Cash Flows (Operating, Investing, Financing) of the discontinuing operation **on the face of P&L / Notes**; restate prior period comparatives. |
| **AS 25: Interim Financial Reporting** | Apply the **same accounting policies** as annual financial statements. Measure income tax using the **Estimated Average Annual Effective Tax Rate** applied to interim pre-tax income. | **Inventory write-down to NRV** in Quarter 1 can be reversed in Quarter 3 **only up to the original write-down amount** if NRV recovers within the same financial year. |
| **AS 26: Intangible Assets** | Identifiable, non-monetary asset without physical substance.<br>**Research Phase**: Expense 100% to P&L.<br>**Development Phase**: Capitalize ONLY after technical & commercial feasibility criteria are met. | **Internally generated Goodwill, Brands, Mastheads, Publishing Titles, and Customer Lists** can **NEVER** be recognized as intangible assets. Presumptive max amortization period is **10 years** on a straight-line basis. |
| **AS 27: Financial Reporting of Interests in Joint Ventures** | **3 Forms**: Jointly Controlled Operations (JCO), Jointly Controlled Assets (JCA), Jointly Controlled Entities (JCE). | In **Consolidated Financial Statements**, report interest in a **JCE** using **Proportionate Consolidation** (combine venturer's share of each asset, liability, income & expense line-by-line). |
| **AS 28: Impairment of Assets** | **Impairment Loss** = Carrying Amount - Recoverable Amount, where Recoverable Amount = Higher of Net Selling Price and Value in Use. | Allocate **CGU Impairment Loss** in strict order: **1st to Goodwill** allocated to the CGU, **2nd pro-rata** to other assets of the CGU (never reduce an asset below Higher of NSP, VIU, or Zero). **Goodwill impairment cannot be reversed** unless caused by a specific non-recurring external event! |
| **AS 29: Provisions, Contingent Liabilities & Contingent Assets** | Recognize a **Provision** when: 1) Present obligation from past event, 2) Probable (>50%) outflow of resources, 3) Reliable estimate possible. **Discounting is prohibited** (except decommissioning under AS 10). | **Contingent Assets** are neither recognized nor disclosed in financial statements (mention in **Board's Report** if probable; recognize in P&L only when **Virtually Certain**). **Restructuring provision** requires a detailed formal plan + valid expectation raised before BS date. |
