## 1. BUYBACK OF SECURITIES (SECTIONS 68, 69 & 70 OF COMPANIES ACT, 2013)

Buyback of equity shares represents a capital reduction and payout mechanism governed by strict statutory ceilings under Section 68, capital preservation rules under Section 69, and prohibitions under Section 70.

### 1.1 The 3-Step Statutory Resource Test (Section 68(2))

To determine the maximum permissible number of equity shares a company can buy back at an offer price, all three independent statutory tests must be computed. The maximum permissible buyback is the LEAST of the three results.

| Statutory Test Parameter | Legal Threshold & Formula | Verification & Applicability Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Test 1: Shares Outstanding Limit | Max 25% of total existing paid-up equity shares | Based on number of equity shares only; computed in single FY |
| Test 2: Resource / Net Worth Limit | Max 25% of (Paid-up Capital + Free Reserves + Sec Prem) | Total buyback consideration cannot exceed 25% of net worth |
| Test 3: Post-Buyback Debt-Equity Test | (Total Debt) / (Post-Buyback Equity + Reserves) <= 2:1 | Secured + Unsecured debt / (Cap + Reserves - Buyback) <= 2 |

#### Comprehensive Mathematical Formulation for Debt-Equity Test:
Let $N$ = Number of shares bought back, and $P$ = Offer price per share.
$$\text{Minimum Required Post-Buyback Net Worth} = \frac{\text{Total Debt (Secured + Unsecured)}}{2}$$
$$\text{Maximum Permissible Outlay under Debt-Equity} = \text{Current Net Worth} - \frac{\text{Total Debt}}{2}$$
$$\text{Number of Shares Permissible under Debt-Equity} = \frac{\text{Maximum Permissible Outlay}}{\text{Offer Price (P)} + \text{Nominal Value (if funded from Free Reserves for CRR)}}$$

### 1.2 Capital Redemption Reserve (CRR - Section 69) Transfer Rules

When shares are bought back out of Free Reserves or Securities Premium, an amount equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back must be transferred to the Capital Redemption Reserve (CRR).

| Funding Source for Buyback | CRR Transfer Requirement | Legal Utilization Restriction |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh Issue of Specified Securities | No CRR transfer required up to nominal value of issue | Cannot issue same class of shares for buyback (Sec 68(1)) |
| Free Reserves (General Res, P&L) | Mandatory transfer of 100% nominal value to CRR | CRR can ONLY be used for issuing fully paid bonus shares |
| Securities Premium Account | Mandatory transfer of 100% nominal value to CRR | Permitted under Sec 68; requires CRR for nominal value |

### 1.3 Journal Entry Proforma for Buyback

| Accounting Stage & Event | Debit Account | Credit Account |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Fresh issue of preference shares | Bank Account (Issue proceeds) | Preference Share Capital & Securities Premium |
| 2. Sale of investments for liquidity | Bank Account (Actual proceeds) | Investment Account & P&L (Profit/Loss on sale) |
| 3. Extinguishment liability created | Equity Share Capital (Nominal) & Premium on Buyback | Equity Shareholders Buyback Account (Total Payable) |
| 4. Adjustment of buyback premium | Securities Premium / Free Reserves Account | Premium on Buyback Account |
| 5. Disbursement to shareholders | Equity Shareholders Buyback Account | Bank Account |
| 6. Mandatory Capital Preservation | General Reserve / P&L Balance Account | Capital Redemption Reserve (CRR) Account |

### 1.4 Three Classic ICAI Examiner Traps in Buyback

| Examiner Trap Scenario | Incorrect Treatment (Mark Loss) | Mandatory Correct ICAI Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Trap 1: Partly Paid Equity Shares | Including partly paid shares in 25% outstanding test | Partly paid shares CANNOT be bought back; call money first |
| Trap 2: Securities Premium & Ind AS | Applying Ind AS Sec 133 restrictions to non-Ind AS | AS entities can freely utilize Securities Premium under Sec 68 |
| Trap 3: Cooling-off Period & Authorisation | Assuming Board can authorize 25% buyback anytime | Board can authorize <=10%; >10% to 25% requires Special Resolution |

## 2. SCHEDULE III (DIVISION I) FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

Division I of Schedule III applies to all Non-Ind AS companies preparing financial statements under Companies (Accounting Standards) Rules, 2006.

### 2.1 Operating Cycle & Current vs. Non-Current Classification

An asset or liability is classified as Current if it satisfies any of the four statutory conditions; otherwise, it is Non-Current.

| Balance Sheet Component | Current Classification Criteria | Non-Current Classification Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Operating Cycle Settlement | Expected realization/settlement within normal operating cycle | Realization/settlement exceeds normal operating cycle |
| 12-Month Rule from Balance Sheet | Due for realization/settlement within 12 months of reporting date | Settlement period extends beyond 12 months of reporting date |
| Trading & Cash Equivalents | Held primarily for trading; unrestricted cash/cash equivalent | Restricted cash (>12 months) or strategic long-term hold |
| Unconditional Right to Defer | No unconditional right to defer settlement for >= 12 months | Entity has unconditional right to defer settlement >= 12M |

### 2.2 Contingent Liabilities vs. Commitments (Notes to Accounts)

Schedule III mandates strict itemized disclosure in the Notes to Accounts for items not recognized in the Balance Sheet.

| Obligation Type | Balance Sheet Treatment | Mandatory Disclosure Schedule |
|---|---|---|
| Claims against Co. not acknowledged as debt | Not recognized as liability on Balance Sheet | Disclosed under Contingent Liabilities note (AS 29 & Sch III) |
| Guarantees given to banks on behalf of third parties | Off-balance sheet financial commitment | Disclosed under Contingent Liabilities with maximum exposure |
| Estimated amount of unexecuted capital contracts | Off-balance sheet capital expenditure pipeline | Disclosed under Commitments (Capital Commitments) |
| Uncalled liability on partly paid shares/investments | Obligation contingent upon future calls | Disclosed under Commitments (Other Commitments) |

### 2.3 High-Yield Schedule III Disclosure Mandates

| Line Item / Statutory Requirement | Mandated Disclosure Basis | Key Regulatory Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Trade Payables (MSME ageing schedule) | Ageing: <1 yr, 1-2 yrs, 2-3 yrs, >3 yrs (MSME vs Others) | Specific MSMED Act interest paid/payable disclosure |
| Trade Receivables ageing schedule | Undisputed/Disputed; Good vs Significant Credit Risk | Ageing buckets from transaction/due date (<6M, 6M-1Y, 1-2Y) |
| CWIP & Intangible Assets Under Dev | Ageing schedule (<1 yr, 1-2 yrs, 2-3 yrs, >3 yrs) | Separate disclosure of overdue projects & cost overrun plans |
| 11 Mandatory Financial Ratios | Current, Debt-Equity, DSCR, ROE, Inventory Turnover, etc. | Must explain variance if ratio fluctuates by >25% vs LY |

## 3. INTERNAL RECONSTRUCTION (SECTION 66 OF COMPANIES ACT, 2013)

Internal reconstruction reorganizes financial capital structure without liquidating the company, writing off accumulated losses against sacrificed claims of stakeholders.

### 3.1 Capital Reduction Account Mechanism

The Capital Reduction (or Reconstruction) Account is a temporary nominal account. Sacrifices made by shareholders, debenture holders, and creditors are credited; asset write-downs and loss write-offs are debited.

| Reconstruction Transaction | Debit Account | Credit Account |
|---|---|---|
| Reduction in face value of equity shares | Old Equity Share Capital (Old FV) | New Equity Share Capital (New FV) & Capital Reduction |
| Sacrifice by preference shareholders | Preference Share Capital Account | Capital Reduction Account |
| Forgiveness of claims by creditors/lenders | Sundry Creditors / Debentures Account | Capital Reduction Account |
| Writing off accumulated fictitious losses | Capital Reduction Account | P&L (Dr. balance), Preliminary Expenses, Goodwill |
| Writing down overvalued PPE/Inventories | Capital Reduction Account | Property, Plant & Equipment, Inventory Account |
| Settlement of reconstruction expenses | Capital Reduction Account | Bank Account |
| Final transfer of surplus balance | Capital Reduction Account (Surplus balance) | Capital Reserve Account (Balance Sheet reserve) |

### 3.2 Share Surrender & Re-issue Technique

| Stage in Share Surrender Method | Accounting Entry Debit | Accounting Entry Credit |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Equity shares surrendered by holders | Equity Share Capital Account | Shares Surrendered Account |
| 2. Re-issue of surrendered shares to creditors | Sundry Creditors / Debentures Account | Shares Surrendered Account |
| 3. Conversion to new denomination/rights | Shares Surrendered Account | Equity / Preference Share Capital Account |
| 4. Cancellation of unissued surrendered shares | Shares Surrendered Account (Unutilized) | Capital Reduction Account |

### 3.3 High-Yield Internal Reconstruction Examiner Pitfalls

| Exam Trap Scenario | Student Mistake | Examiner Correct Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Unrecorded Contingent Liability Paid | Debiting expense or P&L Account | Debit Capital Reduction Account directly (part of scheme) |
| Appreciation in Value of Land/Building | Ignoring unrealized revaluation gains | Credit Capital Reduction Account to absorb extra losses |
| Words "And Reduced" in Name | Omitting suffix from Balance Sheet title | Must add "and Reduced" to company name if directed by NCLT |

## 4. AS 14 ACCOUNTING FOR AMALGAMATIONS

Amalgamations under AS 14 are classified into two distinct categories: Amalgamation in the Nature of Merger and Amalgamation in the Nature of Purchase.

### 4.1 Merger vs. Purchase: The 5 Statutory Conditions (AS 14.3(e))

All five conditions must be simultaneously satisfied for an amalgamation to be classified as a Merger (Pooling of Interests Method). If even one condition fails, it is an Amalgamation in the Nature of Purchase.

| Statutory Condition Parameter | Pooling of Interests (Merger Method) | Purchase Method |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Transfer of Assets & Liabilities | All assets and liabilities must be transferred | Only agreed assets and liabilities are taken over |
| 2. 90% Equity Shareholder Continuity | >= 90% face value holders must become transferee shareholders | Continuity of 90% equity shareholders is not mandatory |
| 3. Consideration Settlement Mode | Wholly in equity shares (cash ONLY for fractions) | Consideration payable in shares, debentures, or cash |
| 4. Continuity of Business | Transferee intends to carry on transferor business | Transferee may discontinue or sell off business segments |
| 5. Book Value Preservation | Recorded at book values (except accounting policy parity) | Recorded at agreed values (fair values) or book values |

### 4.2 Purchase Consideration (PC) Computation: Net Assets vs. Net Payment

| PC Computation Method | Inclusions & Valuation Formula | Strict Exclusions (Examiner Traps) |
|---|---|---|
| Net Payment Method (Primary Method) | Agreed payments to Shareholders (Equity + Pref) in shares/cash | Payments to Debenture holders & Creditors are NEVER PC |
| Net Assets Method (Secondary Method) | Agreed value of Assets taken over LESS Liabilities taken over | Liquidation expenses and un-taken liabilities excluded |
| Intrinsic Value / Share Exchange Ratio | Value per share of Transferor / Value per share of Transferee | Do not compute PC on face value when exchange ratio given |

### 4.3 Amalgamation Adjustment Reserve (Purchase Method)

When an amalgamation is accounted under the Purchase Method, statutory reserves (e.g., Export Profit Reserve, Investment Allowance Reserve) required by statute to be maintained must be preserved.

| Accounting Element | Journal Entry Format | Presentation in Balance Sheet |
|---|---|---|
| Preserving Statutory Reserve | Dr. Amalgamation Adjustment Reserve | Cr. Statutory Reserve Account (Reserves & Surplus) |
| Balance Sheet Presentation | Shown as a negative deduction under Reserves & Surplus | Once statutory condition expires, entry is reversed |
| Goodwill vs. Capital Reserve | PC > Net Assets = Goodwill; PC < Net Assets = Capital Reserve | Goodwill amortized over max 5 years; Capital Reserve kept |

## 5. CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS (AS 21, AS 23 & AS 27)

Consolidation involves combining parent and subsidiary accounts line-by-line under AS 21, equity accounting for associates under AS 23, and proportionate consolidation for joint ventures under AS 27.

### 5.1 Cost of Control / Elimination Matrix (AS 21)

Cost of Control represents the difference between the acquisition cost of the investment and the parent's equity in the net assets of the subsidiary on the date of acquisition.

| Consolidation Element | Computation Formula / Allocation Rule | Balance Sheet Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-Acquisition Profits & Reserves | Reserves & P&L existing on or prior to acquisition date | Capital profit; reduces Cost of Investment (Goodwill/Cap Res) |
| Post-Acquisition Profits & Reserves | Growth in Reserves & P&L after acquisition date to BS date | Revenue profit; added to Consolidated Reserves & P&L |
| Goodwill on Consolidation | Cost of Investment > Parent's share in Pre-acquisition Net Assets | Shown under Intangible Assets on Consolidated Balance Sheet |
| Capital Reserve on Consolidation | Cost of Investment < Parent's share in Pre-acquisition Net Assets | Shown under Reserves & Surplus on Consolidated Balance Sheet |

### 5.2 Minority Interest (Non-Controlling Interest)

| Minority Interest Component | Equity Allocation Principle | Presentation Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Paid-up Share Capital | Minority % x Face Value of Subsidiary Equity Capital | Shown separately outside Shareholders' Funds |
| Share in Pre-Acquisition Reserves | Minority % x Pre-Acquisition Capital Reserves & P&L | Added to Minority Interest total on Balance Sheet |
| Share in Post-Acquisition Profits | Minority % x Post-Acquisition Revenue Reserves & P&L | Added to Minority Interest total on Balance Sheet |
| Preference Share Capital in Subsidiary | 100% of Preference Capital held by outsiders + arrears dividend | Included within Minority Interest aggregate liability |

### 5.3 Elimination of Intra-Group Balances & Unrealized Profits

| Intra-Group Transaction Type | Downstream Sale (Holding -> Sub) | Upstream Sale (Sub -> Holding) |
|---|---|---|
| Mutual Indebtedness (Debtors/Creditors) | Eliminate 100% mutual balance from Debtors & Creditors | Eliminate 100% mutual balance from Debtors & Creditors |
| Unrealized Profit in Ending Inventory | Eliminate 100% unrealized profit from Consolidated P&L | Eliminate 100% from Inventory; allocate to Parent & Minority |
| Unrealized Profit in Fixed Assets | Eliminate 100% profit from Asset and Consolidated P&L | Adjust asset value and subsequent excess depreciation |

## 6. BRANCH ACCOUNTING & FOREIGN BRANCHES (AS 11)

### 6.1 Integral vs. Non-Integral Foreign Operations (AS 11)

| Parameter / Indicator | Integral Foreign Operation (IFO) | Non-Integral Foreign Operation (NFO) |
|---|---|---|
| Nature of Operation | Carries on business as an integral extension of Head Office | Operates with significant commercial & financial autonomy |
| Financing & Cash Flow | Financed by HO; remittances directly impact HO cash flows | Financed by local borrowings; self-sustaining operations |
| Translation Difference Treatment | Exchange difference recognized immediately in P&L Account | Accumulated in Foreign Currency Translation Reserve (FCTR) |

### 6.2 AS 11 Foreign Currency Translation Rates Matrix

| Trial Balance Line Item | Integral Foreign Operation Rate | Non-Integral Foreign Operation Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Opening & Closing Inventory | Rate on date of opening/closing inventory valuation | Closing Rate on Balance Sheet date |
| Fixed Assets (PPE) & Depreciation | Historical Rate on date of acquisition of asset | Closing Rate on Balance Sheet date |
| Monetary Assets (Debtors, Cash, B/R) | Closing Rate on Balance Sheet date | Closing Rate on Balance Sheet date |
| Monetary Liabilities (Creditors, B/P) | Closing Rate on Balance Sheet date | Closing Rate on Balance Sheet date |
| Revenue Income & Expenses | Average Rate for period (or Transaction Rate) | Average Rate for period (or Transaction Rate) |
| Head Office Account & Remittances | Actual recorded book balance in Head Office books | Actual recorded book balance in Head Office books |

# PART B: MASTER 3-COLUMN ACCOUNTING STANDARDS MATRIX
## Complete Standards Coverage (AS 1 to AS 29) for CA Intermediate (New Scheme)

Every entry is formatted in the strict 3-column architecture (Standard & Title, Recognition Rule, Disclosure & Trap):

| AS No. & Title (Scope) | Recognition & Measurement Rule (Core Threshold/Formula) | Key Disclosure & Examiner Trap |
|---|---|---|
| AS 1: Disclosure of Accounting Policies | Fundamental assumptions: Going Concern, Consistency, Accrual | Disclosure required ONLY if fundamental assumptions NOT followed |
| AS 2: Valuation of Inventories | Lower of Cost and Net Realizable Value (NRV) item-by-item | Cost excludes abnormal waste, storage, and admin overheads |
| AS 3: Cash Flow Statements | Classify flows into Operating, Investing, and Financing | Non-cash transactions excluded; interest/dividend policy fixed |
| AS 4: Contingencies & Events Post-BS | Adjusting events: conditions existing on BS date; Non-adjusting: post-BS | Proposed dividend post-BS is non-adjusting (disclose in notes) |
| AS 5: Net Profit/Loss, Prior Period & Policy | Separate disclosure for Prior Period items and Exceptional items | Change in depreciation method is a Change in Accounting Estimate |
| AS 7: Construction Contracts | Percentage of Completion Method (POCM) based on contract cost ratio | Expected loss recognized IMMEDIATELY in full regardless of POCM |
| AS 9: Revenue Recognition | Significant risks and rewards transferred; measurable consideration | Consignment sales recognized ONLY when sold to third party |
| AS 10: Property, Plant & Equipment | Cost model or Revaluation model; component-wise depreciation | Admin and start-up costs excluded; dismantling cost capitalized |
| AS 11: Foreign Exchange Rates | Monetary items at Closing Rate; Non-monetary at Historical Rate | Exchange differences on revenue items charged to P&L |
| AS 12: Government Grants | Capital approach for asset grants; Income approach for revenue grants | Asset grant: deduct from gross asset or treat as deferred income |
| AS 13: Accounting for Investments | Current: lower of cost or fair value; Long-term: cost less permanent drop | Reclassification from Current to Long-term at lower of cost and FV |
| AS 14: Accounting for Amalgamations | Merger (Pooling of Interests) vs Purchase Method | Amalgamation Adjustment Reserve created ONLY in Purchase method |
| AS 15: Employee Benefits | Defined Contribution vs Defined Benefit (Actuarial Valuation) | Short-term compensated absences recognized on undiscounted basis |
| AS 16: Borrowing Costs | Capitalize qualifying asset costs during active development period | Suspension of capitalization during extended inactive periods |
| AS 17: Segment Reporting | 10% threshold: Revenue, Result (P/L), or Assets; 75% external revenue | Unallocated corporate expenses excluded from segment result |
| AS 18: Related Party Disclosures | Control, Significant Influence, KMP, and relatives of KMP | No disclosure required if state-controlled enterprise transactions |
| AS 19: Leases | Finance Lease if risks/rewards transferred; else Operating Lease | Finance lease asset and liability recorded at lower of FV and PV of MLP |
| AS 20: Earnings Per Share | Basic EPS = Net Profit / WANES; Diluted EPS adjusts dilutive potentials | Contingently issuable shares included ONLY when conditions met |
| AS 21: Consolidated Financial Statements | Parent-subsidiary line-by-line consolidation (Cost of Control) | 100% unrealized downstream profit eliminated against group P&L |
| AS 22: Taxes on Income | Recognize DTA/DTL on timing differences (Taxable vs Accounting) | DTA on unabsorbed loss recognized ONLY if virtual certainty |
| AS 23: Investments in Associates (CFS) | Equity Method: Cost + Post-acquisition share of profits - Dividends | Goodwill/Capital Reserve identified on date of associate acquisition |
| AS 24: Discontinuing Operations | Initial Disclosure Event: binding sale agreement or approved plan | Distinct operational segment; disclosures continue until abandonment |
| AS 25: Interim Financial Reporting | Discrete vs Integral view; recognize revenues/costs as annual | Same accounting policies as annual statements; no seasonal smoothing |
| AS 26: Intangible Assets | Identifiable non-monetary asset without physical substance (Cost model) | Internally generated goodwill/brands CANNOT be recognized |
| AS 27: Financial Reporting in JVs | Jointly Controlled Operations, Assets, and Entities (Proportionate) | Proportionate consolidation used in CFS; single-line equity in SFS |
| AS 28: Impairment of Assets | Impairment Loss = Carrying Amount LESS Recoverable Amount | Recoverable Amount = Higher of Value in Use and Net Selling Price |
| AS 29: Provisions & Contingent Assets | Provision: present obligation from past event, probable outflow | Contingent assets NEVER recognized in books (disclose in Report) |
