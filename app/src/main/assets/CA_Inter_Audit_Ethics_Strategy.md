## CHAPTER 1: NATURE, OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE OF AUDIT (SA 200, SA 210 & SQC 1)

| Col 1: Standard / Section | Col 2: Core Rule / Threshold / Requirement | Col 3: Exception / Examiner Trap / Key Proviso |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SA 200: Overall Objectives** | Obtain **reasonable assurance** that financial statements are free from **material misstatement**. | Reasonable assurance is **high, not absolute** assurance due to **inherent limitations of audit**. |
| **SA 200: Professional Skepticism** | Maintain **questioning mind** and critical assessment of audit evidence throughout audit. | Skepticism does NOT mean assuming management is dishonest, nor does it guarantee discovering all frauds. |
| **SA 200: Ethical Requirements** | Compliance with Code of Ethics: **Integrity, Objectivity, Due Care, Confidentiality, Behaviour**. | **Objectivity** is compromised by conflict of interest, bias, or undue influence of others. |
| **SA 210: Preconditions for Audit** | Determine acceptable **FRF** and obtain management's agreement on **fundamental responsibilities**. | If preconditions are absent, auditor **must refuse** engagement unless required by law. |
| **SA 210: Engagement Letter** | Covers **objective, scope, responsibilities, FRF**, and expected form of reports. | Recurring audits do **not** need new letter annually unless there is a **change in scope/ownership/terms**. |
| **SA 210: Change in Terms** | Reasonable justification required (e.g., change in circumstances vs limitation on scope). | If management refuses to continue original terms, auditor must **withdraw and report** to TCWG/regulators. |
| **SQC 1 / SA 220: Quality Control** | System of quality control for firms: **Leadership, Ethics, Acceptance, HR, Engagement, Monitoring**. | **Engagement Quality Control Review (EQCR)** mandatory for listed entities prior to report issuance. |
| **SQC 1: Retention Period** | Audit documentation must be retained for at least **7 years** from date of auditor's report. | Ownership of working papers belongs to the **auditor**; client has no right of lien over audit files. |

## CHAPTER 2: AUDIT STRATEGY, AUDIT PLANNING & AUDIT PROGRAMME (SA 300)

| Col 1: Standard / Concept | Col 2: Core Rule / Threshold / Requirement | Col 3: Exception / Examiner Trap / Key Proviso |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SA 300: Audit Strategy** | Sets **scope, timing, and direction** of audit; guides development of detailed audit plan. | Strategy is established **before** the audit plan, but strategy and planning are **interrelated and iterative**. |
| **SA 300: Audit Plan** | Detailed operational plan including nature, timing & extent (**NTE**) of RAP and further procedures. | More detailed than strategy; must include risk assessment procedures and planned tests of controls/substantive. |
| **SA 300: Continuous Planning** | Planning is **not a discrete phase**; it continues throughout the engagement as facts emerge. | Changes to strategy/plan must be **documented** along with reasons for significant modifications. |
| **Audit Programme** | Written plan containing detailed audit instructions for engagement assistants to verify assertions. | Avoid making programme too rigid; team must report **unforeseen matters** rather than blindly checking boxes. |
| **Direction & Supervision** | Partner must supervise team based on **capabilities, size, complexity, and assessed RMM**. | Inexperienced team members require closer supervision and **100% review** of high-risk work papers. |

## CHAPTER 3: RISK ASSESSMENT & INTERNAL CONTROL (SA 315, SA 320 & SA 330)

| Col 1: Standard / Concept | Col 2: Core Rule / Threshold / Requirement | Col 3: Exception / Examiner Trap / Key Proviso |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SA 315: Identifying & Assessing RMM** | Identify RMM at **Financial Statement Level** and **Assertion Level** via understanding entity. | RMM consists of **Inherent Risk (IR)** and **Control Risk (CR)**; Detection Risk (DR) is controllable by auditor. |
| **SA 315: Components of Internal Control** | 5 Components: **Control Environment, Risk Assessment, Info System, Control Activities, Monitoring**. | Strong control environment does **not** substitute for performing substantive procedures on material items. |
| **SA 315: Significant Risks** | Risks requiring special audit consideration (fraud risk, complex/related party transactions). | If a risk is **Significant**, the auditor **cannot rely** on controls tested in prior years; must test in current year! |
| **SA 320: Materiality in Audit** | Set materiality for FS as a whole based on **benchmark** (e.g., 5% PBT, 1% Revenue, 1% Total Assets). | Materiality is based on user needs, **not** audit risk. Professional judgment applies; quantitative & qualitative. |
| **SA 320: Performance Materiality** | Set below overall materiality to reduce risk that aggregate uncorrected misstatements exceed materiality. | Never test items only down to overall materiality; use performance materiality to avoid aggregation risk! |
| **SA 330: Auditor's Responses to Risk** | Overall responses at FS level (skepticism, experienced staff) + NTE of further audit procedures. | **Test of Controls (TOC)** mandatory if substantive alone cannot provide sufficient appropriate audit evidence (SAAE). |
| **SA 265: Deficiencies in Internal Control** | Communicate **Significant Deficiencies** in writing to **TCWG** within reasonable time period. | Communicating deficiencies does NOT absolve auditor from designing adequate substantive procedures. |

## CHAPTER 4: AUDIT EVIDENCE (SA 500, SA 501, SA 505, SA 510, SA 520, SA 530 & SA 550)

| Col 1: Standard / Procedure | Col 2: Core Rule / Threshold / Requirement | Col 3: Exception / Examiner Trap / Key Proviso |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SA 500: Audit Evidence** | Must obtain **Sufficient Appropriate Audit Evidence (SAAE)**: Sufficiency = quantity; Appropriateness = quality. | External, written, direct evidence is **more reliable** than internal, oral, indirect evidence. |
| **SA 501: Inventory Physical Verification** | Auditor **must attend physical inventory count** unless practically impracticable. | If attendance impracticable, perform **alternative procedures** (inspect subsequent sales); if not possible, **modify opinion**. |
| **SA 501: Litigation & Claims** | Direct communication with entity's **external legal counsel** via letter of general/specific inquiry. | If management refuses permission or lawyer refuses to respond, auditor must **modify audit opinion**. |
| **SA 505: External Confirmations** | Positive (must respond) vs Negative (respond only if disagree) confirmation requests. | **Negative confirmation** provides less persuasive evidence; allowed ONLY when RMM is low, large population & low error rate. |
| **SA 505: Management Refusal** | Inquire into reasons; evaluate impact on RMM; perform alternative audit procedures. | If refusal is **unreasonable** or alternative evidence unobtainable, **modify opinion under SA 705**! |
| **SA 510: Initial Audit Engagements** | Verify opening balances: determine whether correct, reflect consistent policies, or need disclosure. | If prior year audited by predecessor, review working papers or evaluate if current procedures provide evidence. |
| **SA 520: Analytical Procedures** | Mandatory at **Planning** (Risk Assessment) and **Overall Review** stage; optional as Substantive procedure. | Substantive analytical procedures require predictable relationships; cannot rely solely on analytics for unique risks. |
| **SA 530: Audit Sampling** | Sample design: **Statistical** (random, systematic) vs **Non-Statistical**; every unit must have chance. | **Tolerable Misstatement** must be $\le$ Performance Materiality. Project sample errors to total population! |
| **SA 550: Related Parties** | Inquire into identity, nature, business rationale; inspect contracts outside normal course of business. | Undisclosed related party transactions trigger **presumption of fraud risk**; re-evaluate control assessment! |

## CHAPTER 5: AUDIT OF ITEMS OF FINANCIAL STATEMENTS (ASSERTION-BASED SUBSTANTIVE TESTING)

| Col 1: Balance Sheet / P&L Item | Col 2: Core Assertion Verified | Col 3: Key Audit Procedure & Examiner Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Share Capital** | Completeness, Rights & Obligations | Verify authorized/paid-up capital with MCA Portal (Form PAS-3, SH-7); check board & shareholder resolutions. |
| **Long-Term Borrowings** | Completeness & Valuation | Direct bank confirmation; verify covenants, mortgage charges registered with ROC within **30 days** (Form CHG-1). |
| **Trade Payables & MSME** | Completeness (Cut-off & Unrecorded) | Test **subsequent payments** in post-BS period; verify MSME ageing and calculate interest payable under Sec 16 MSMED. |
| **Property, Plant & Equipment (PPE)** | Existence, Ownership & Valuation | Physical verification on sample basis; inspect title deeds; test depreciation rates against **Schedule II useful lives**. |
| **Trade Receivables** | Existence & Valuation (Recoverability) | Send **SA 505 external confirmations**; verify ageing schedule; examine provisioning for ECL / doubtful debts. |
| **Cash and Bank Balances** | Existence & Valuation | Surprise cash count; obtain independent **Bank Confirmation Letters**; inspect **Bank Reconciliation Statements (BRS)**. |
| **Sales / Revenue Recognition** | Occurrence, Cut-off & Measurement | Match invoices with dispatch proofs (LR/e-way bills); perform **cut-off testing** (5 days pre & post BS date); check AS 9/115. |
| **Purchases & Direct Expenses** | Occurrence & Completeness | Match Purchase Orders, Goods Receipt Notes (GRN), and Supplier Invoices; test pre-and-post BS cut-off. |
| **Contingent Liabilities** | Completeness & Disclosure | Inspect legal expense ledger, Board minutes, tax assessment orders; ensure full disclosure in Notes to Accounts. |

### MODULE 1: TOP 5 EXAMINER TRAPS & PRESENTATION CHECKLIST
1. **Trap 1 (SA 501 Attendance)**: Stating that an auditor can skip physical inventory count because of "distance or inconvenience". *Correction*: Inconvenience is NEVER a valid ground; only physical impossibility (e.g., hazardous warehouse in war zone) qualifies, requiring alternative tests.
2. **Trap 2 (SA 505 Negative Confirmations)**: Relying on non-reply of negative confirmations as absolute proof of correctness. *Correction*: Non-reply may mean the debtor never received the letter; negative confirmations are persuasive ONLY when RMM is very low and controls are proven strong.
3. **Trap 3 (SA 320 Aggregation Risk)**: Testing transactions against overall materiality instead of performance materiality. *Correction*: Multiple uncorrected errors below overall materiality aggregate to exceed it; performance materiality provides the required safety margin.
4. **Trap 4 (SA 530 Sample Evaluation)**: Omitting sample error projection. *Correction*: When anomalies/errors are found in a sample, the auditor MUST project errors to the entire population before concluding.
5. **Trap 5 (MSME Disclosure)**: Forgetting interest under Section 16 of MSMED Act. *Correction*: Always verify whether penal interest @ 3 times the RBI bank rate compounded monthly is computed and disallowed in tax computations.

## CHAPTER 6 & 7: AUDIT DOCUMENTATION, FRAUD & COMPLETION (SA 230, SA 240, SA 560 & SA 570)

| Col 1: Standard / Concept | Col 2: Core Rule / Threshold / Requirement | Col 3: Exception / Examiner Trap / Key Proviso |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SA 230: Audit Documentation** | Working papers must enable an **experienced auditor** with no prior connection to understand the audit. | Assembly of final audit file must be completed within **60 days** from date of auditor's report (SQC 1). |
| **SA 240: Auditor's Fraud Responsibilities** | Primary responsibility for fraud prevention lies with **TCWG & Management**; auditor assesses fraud risk. | Auditor must treat **Revenue Recognition** and **Management Override of Controls** as presumed fraud risks! |
| **SA 240: Fraud Reporting (Sec 143(12))** | If fraud involves **>= Rs. 1 Crore**: Report to **Central Government (MCA)** within **45 days** (Form ADT-4). | If fraud involves **< Rs. 1 Crore**: Report to Audit Committee / Board within **2 days**; disclose in Board's Report. |
| **SA 560: Subsequent Events** | **Type 1 (Adjusting)**: Conditions existed at BS date $\rightarrow$ adjust FS.<br>**Type 2 (Non-adjusting)**: Disclose in Notes. | Events after report date but before FS issued: If management amends, audit amendment & issue dual-dated report. |
| **SA 570: Going Concern** | Assess management's use of going concern assumption for at least **12 months** from balance sheet date. | If Material Uncertainty exists but is adequately disclosed: Issue **Unmodified Opinion + Separate "MURGC" Paragraph**. |
| **SA 570: Inadequate GC Disclosure** | If Material Uncertainty exists and management **fails to disclose** adequately in Notes $\rightarrow$ **Qualified / Adverse**. | If Going Concern basis is **inappropriate** and FS prepared on going concern basis $\rightarrow$ issue **Adverse Opinion**! |
| **SA 580: Written Representations** | Formal written statement from management confirming fulfillment of responsibilities. | Written representations are **supporting evidence, NOT a substitute** for other primary audit evidence! |

## CHAPTER 8: AUDIT REPORT & TYPES OF OPINIONS (SA 700, SA 701, SA 705 & SA 706)

| Col 1: Standard / Section | Col 2: Core Rule / Threshold / Requirement | Col 3: Exception / Examiner Trap / Key Proviso |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SA 700: Forming an Opinion** | Standard unqualified report structure: Title, Addressee, **Opinion Section first**, Basis for Opinion. | Opinion and Basis for Opinion sections must appear at the **very beginning** of the auditor's report. |
| **SA 701: Key Audit Matters (KAM)** | Matters of most significance in audit of current period; selected from matters communicated to TCWG. | **Mandatory for Listed Entities**; KAM does NOT substitute for modified opinion or SA 570 going concern reporting! |
| **SA 705: Qualified Opinion** | Misstatement is **Material but NOT Pervasive** (or unable to obtain SAAE, but effect is not pervasive). | State "Except for the effects of the matter described in Basis for Qualified Opinion paragraph...". |
| **SA 705: Adverse Opinion** | Misstatements are both **Material AND Pervasive** to the financial statements as a whole. | State that financial statements "do not give a true and fair view in conformity with the accounting principles...". |
| **SA 705: Disclaimer of Opinion** | Auditor **unable to obtain SAAE** and possible effects are both **Material AND Pervasive**. | State "we do not express an opinion..."; omit Scope of Audit paragraph when disclaiming opinion. |
| **SA 706: Emphasis of Matter (EOM)** | Draws attention to a matter **appropriately presented/disclosed in FS** that is fundamental to user's understanding. | Cannot be used for matters required to be reported under **SA 701 (KAM)** or matters requiring opinion modification! |
| **SA 706: Other Matter (OM) Paragraph** | Refers to matters **other than those presented in FS** relevant to users' understanding of audit/responsibilities. | Example: Prior period financial statements were audited by another predecessor auditor or were unaudited. |

## CHAPTER 9: COMPANY AUDIT & STATUTORY APPOINTMENTS (COMPANIES ACT, 2013)

| Col 1: Section / Rule | Col 2: Core Rule / Threshold / Requirement | Col 3: Exception / Examiner Trap / Key Proviso |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sec 139(1): Auditor Appointment** | Appointed at 1st AGM; holds office from conclusion of that AGM until conclusion of **6th AGM** (5-year term). | Ratification of auditor appointment at every intervening AGM is **omitted by Companies Amendment Act**! |
| **Sec 139(2): Rotation of Auditors** | Individual: **1 term of 5 consecutive years**; Audit Firm: **2 terms of 5 consecutive years** (Cooling period = 5 years). | Applies to: All Listed Cos; Unlisted Public Cos (Capital >= Rs. 10 Cr); Private Cos (Capital >= Rs. 50 Cr); Borrowings >= Rs. 50 Cr. |
| **Sec 139(6): First Auditor (Non-Govt)** | Appointed by **Board within 30 days** of registration; failing which, EGM appoints within **90 days**. | Holds office until the conclusion of the **1st Annual General Meeting (AGM)** only. |
| **Sec 139(7): First Auditor (Govt Co)** | Appointed by **CAG within 60 days**; if CAG fails, Board within **30 days**; if Board fails, EGM within **60 days**. | Holds office until conclusion of 1st AGM. Govt company = $\ge 51\%$ paid-up capital held by Central/State Govt. |
| **Sec 139(8): Casual Vacancy** | Non-Govt: Board fills within **30 days**; if caused by **Resignation**, approved at GM within **3 months**. | Resigning auditor must file **Form ADT-3** within **30 days** with company, ROC & CAG (penalty min Rs. 50,000). |
| **Sec 140(1): Removal before Expiry** | Requires **Board resolution**, Central Govt approval (**Form ADT-2** within 30 days), and **Special Resolution**. | Special Resolution must be passed within **60 days** of receipt of approval of Central Government. |
| **Sec 141(3)(d): Disqualifications** | Holding security/interest in company/subsidiary/holding/associate: **Prohibited for partner/relative**. | Relative may hold securities of face value not exceeding **Rs. 1,00,000**; 60-day corrective action period! |
| **Sec 141(3)(d)(ii): Indebtedness** | Indebted to company/subsidiary/holding/associate in excess of **Rs. 5,00,000** $\rightarrow$ Disqualified! | Relative indebtedness limit is also **Rs. 5,00,000** (Guarantee/security given limit is **Rs. 1,00,000**). |
| **Sec 141(3)(e): Business Relationship** | Direct/indirect business relationship with company or its group $\rightarrow$ Disqualified! | Commercial transactions on **arm's length basis** in ordinary course of business (e.g. air ticket, hotel) are allowed. |
| **Sec 141(3)(g): Ceiling on Audits** | Max **20 company audits** per partner (full-time). | Excluded from 20-audit limit: **One Person Companies (OPC), Dormant Cos, Small Cos, Private Cos (paid capital < Rs. 100 Cr)**. |
| **Sec 144: Prohibited Services** | Auditor cannot provide: **Accounting/bookkeeping, Internal Audit, Financial Info System, Actuarial, Investment banking**. | Applies to auditor, partners, holding company, subsidiaries, or associate entities! |

## CARO 2020: MASTER 21-CLAUSE REPORTING CHEAT SHEET (ORDER 2020)

| Col 1: CARO 2020 Clause | Col 2: Core Reporting Requirement | Col 3: Threshold & Examiner Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clause 3(i): Property, Plant & Equipment** | Maintenance of proper records, physical verification (3-year cycle), **Title deeds held in company's name**. | If title deeds of immovable property NOT in company's name, disclose dispute, promoter holding & gross value. |
| **Clause 3(ii): Inventory & Working Capital** | Physical verification of inventory; coverage & procedure reasonable; material discrepancies ($\ge 10\%$). | Quarterly returns filed with banks for sanctioned working capital limits **> Rs. 5 Crores** must agree with books! |
| **Clause 3(iii): Investments, Loans & Guarantees** | Investments, loans given, guarantees to any party; terms not prejudicial; schedule of repayment regular. | Report overdue amount **> 90 days** and whether reasonable steps taken; report loans renewed/rolled over! |
| **Clause 3(iv): Loans to Directors (Sec 185/186)** | Compliance with Section 185 (loans to directors) and Section 186 (inter-corporate loans and investments). | If non-compliant, state details of non-compliance, amount involved, and parties to whom loans were given. |
| **Clause 3(v): Public Deposits** | Directives issued by RBI and provisions of Sections 73 to 76 of Companies Act, 2013 and Rules. | If deemed deposits taken, verify compliance with NCLT orders and credit rating requirements. |
| **Clause 3(vi): Cost Records** | Maintenance of cost records prescribed by Central Govt under Section 148(1) of the Act. | Auditor only verifies whether cost accounts are **made and maintained**; detailed cost audit is NOT done! |
| **Clause 3(vii): Statutory Dues** | Undisputed statutory dues unpaid for **> 6 months** from due date as on Balance Sheet date. | Disputed dues: Disclose forum where dispute is pending, amount, and year to which it relates. |
| **Clause 3(viii): Unrecorded Income (Surrendered)** | Transactions not recorded in books surrendered/disclosed in tax assessments under Income-tax Act. | Disclose whether previously unrecorded income has been properly recorded in the books during the year. |
| **Clause 3(ix): Default in Repayment of Borrowings** | Default in repayment of loans/borrowings to banks, financial institutions, or government. | Disclose period and amount of default; declare whether company is a declared **Wilful Defaulter**. |
| **Clause 3(x): Utilization of Funds (IPO/FPO)** | Money raised by Initial Public Offer, FPO, or preferential allotment/private placement u/s 42. | Report whether funds were applied for the purpose for which raised; report deviations and delays. |
| **Clause 3(xi): Fraud Reporting** | Any fraud by the company or on the company noticed or reported during the year. | Report whether **Form ADT-4** filed with Central Govt; report whistle-blower complaints considered! |
| **Clause 3(xii): Nidhi Company** | Net Owned Funds to Deposits ratio (1:20); unencumbered term deposits ($\ge 10\%$). | Report default in payment of interest on deposits or debentures for any period. |
| **Clause 3(xiii): Related Party Transactions** | Compliance with Section 177 (Audit Committee) and Section 188 of Companies Act, 2013. | Details disclosed in Financial Statements as required by applicable accounting standards (AS 18). |
| **Clause 3(xiv): Internal Audit System** | Company has internal audit system commensurate with the size and nature of its business. | Statutory auditor must consider the reports of the Internal Auditors for the period under audit. |
| **Clause 3(xv): Non-Cash Transactions (Sec 192)** | Non-cash transactions with directors or connected persons (Section 192 compliance). | Prior approval by resolution in General Meeting mandatory; valuation by Registered Valuer required. |
| **Clause 3(xvi): RBI Registration (NBFC - Sec 45-IA)** | Registration required under Section 45-IA of Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. | Report whether company conducts Core Investment Company (CIC) activities; number of CICs in group. |
| **Clause 3(xvii): Cash Losses** | Amount of **Cash Losses** incurred in current financial year and immediately preceding financial year. | Cash Loss = Net Profit/Loss after tax excluding non-cash expenses like depreciation and amortization. |
| **Clause 3(xviii): Resignation of Statutory Auditor** | Consider issues, objections, or concerns raised by the outgoing statutory auditor. | Incoming auditor must obtain written communication and evaluate grounds for auditor resignation. |
| **Clause 3(xix): Financial Viability (Material Uncertainty)** | Evaluation of ageing, financial ratios, expected realisation of financial assets within **1 year**. | Report whether auditor is of the opinion that no material uncertainty exists regarding capability to meet liabilities! |
| **Clause 3(xx): Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)** | Transfer of unspent CSR funds under Section 135(5) and ongoing project funds under Section 135(6). | Unspent fund (non-ongoing) must be transferred to Fund specified in Sch VII within **6 months** of FY end! |
| **Clause 3(xxi): Qualifications in Group Entities** | Qualifications/adverse remarks in CARO reports of companies included in Consolidated Financial Statements. | State details of companies and the paragraph numbers of the CARO report containing adverse remarks. |

## CHAPTER 10 & 11: AUDIT OF BANKS & SPECIAL ENTITIES

| Col 1: Parameter / Entity | Col 2: Core Rule / Threshold / Requirement | Col 3: Exception / Examiner Trap / Key Proviso |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bank Audit: NPA Classification** | Interest/installment overdue for **> 90 days** for term loans; out of order for CC/OD. | Cash Credit / Overdraft out of order if balance exceeds sanctioned limit or no credits for 90 days. |
| **Bank Audit: Agricultural Loans** | Short duration crop: overdue for **2 crop seasons**; Long duration crop: overdue for **1 crop season**. | Long duration crops are crops with crop season longer than 1 year (e.g., sugarcane, rubber). |
| **Bank Audit: Substandard Asset** | NPA for a period $\le 12\text{ months}$; general provision = **15%** (unsecured = 25%). | Infrastructure lending with escrow accounts has unsecured provisioning of 20%. |
| **Bank Audit: Doubtful Asset** | Remains substandard for $> 12\text{ months}$. Provision: **D1 (<=1 yr): 25%; D2 (1-3 yr): 40%; D3 (>3 yr): 100%**. | Unsecured portion of doubtful asset attracts **100% provisioning**, regardless of D1/D2/D3 duration! |
| **Bank Audit: Loss Asset** | Asset identified as uncollectible by bank, internal/external auditors, or RBI inspection. | **100% Provisioning** required immediately on the entire carrying amount. |
| **Government Audit: Roles of CAG** | Audit of receipts, expenditure, stores & stock, and government commercial enterprises. | **Propriety Audit**: Examines whether expenditure is sanctioned by rules, economical, and prudent. |
| **NGO Audit: Internal Checks** | Direct foreign contributions regulated under **FCRA, 2010**; separate dedicated bank account mandatory. | Verify donor restrictions; funds earmarked for specific projects cannot be mixed with general revenue. |
| **Hospital Audit** | Audit fees, patient charges, concession register, pharmacy inventory, and capital grant utilization. | Verify collection reconciliation between billing software and physical bank deposits daily. |
| **Club Audit** | Entrance fees (capitalized vs revenue), subscription arrears, bar sales, guest fees. | Subscription in arrears cannot be recognized indefinitely if collectability is doubtful. |

### MODULE 2: TOP 5 EXAMINER TRAPS & PRESENTATION CHECKLIST
1. **Trap 1 (Relative Holding Shares u/s 141(3)(d))**: Concluding an auditor is disqualified because a relative holds Rs. 80,000 face value of shares. *Correction*: Relatives are permitted to hold shares of face value up to **Rs. 1,00,000**. Even if it exceeds Rs. 1,00,000, there is a **60-day corrective window** before disqualification triggers.
2. **Trap 2 (CARO Clause 3(ii) Working Capital)**: Checking only the sanction letter of working capital. *Correction*: The trigger is sanctioned limit **> Rs. 5 Crores** from banks on the basis of security of current assets; the auditor must specifically reconcile quarterly stock statements with books.
3. **Trap 3 (Bank NPA Unsecured Provision)**: Applying 25% or 40% provision to the unsecured portion of a Doubtful Asset. *Correction*: The unsecured portion of ANY doubtful asset attracts **100% provision**; the percentage (25%/40%/100%) applies ONLY to the secured portion!
4. **Trap 4 (Sec 143(12) Fraud Reporting)**: Reporting a fraud of Rs. 80 Lakhs to the Central Government. *Correction*: Frauds below **Rs. 1 Crore** are reported ONLY to the Board / Audit Committee; only frauds of **Rs. 1 Crore and above** are reported to the Central Government in Form ADT-4.
5. **Trap 5 (Going Concern Reporting)**: Issuing an adverse opinion just because a material uncertainty exists. *Correction*: If material uncertainty is adequately disclosed in Notes, the opinion remains **Unmodified**, accompanied by an independent **Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern (MURGC)** paragraph.
